About

A roastery with a café attached

Kestrel started in 2015 with a used 5 kg roaster, one wholesale account, and a lease we probably couldn't afford. Not much has changed except the roaster and the number of accounts.

Our story

Eleven years on the same corner

We opened on Weller Street because the rent was cheap and the light was good. The first year we roasted at night and served coffee during the day, which was exhausting and, in hindsight, the best possible way to learn.

These days there's a proper roasting schedule, a team of nine, and a production room in the back that you can watch through the glass. The espresso machine is the same one we bought secondhand in 2015. We've been told we should replace it. We won't.

What hasn't changed is the approach: buy carefully, roast lightly, sell it fresh, and treat the people who walk in like people who walked in — not transactions with legs.

FAQ

Common questions

How fresh is the coffee, really?

We roast every Tuesday and bags hit the shelf Thursday. Nothing sits longer than three weeks — if it does, it goes to staff or the compost, not to you. The roast date is printed on every bag so you can check for yourself.

Do you grind beans in the shop?

Yes, free of charge. Tell us your brew method — espresso, V60, French press, Aeropress, drip — and we'll dial the grind to match. We'd gently suggest whole bean if you have a grinder at home; it makes more difference than people expect.

Can I work from the café?

Absolutely. There's free Wi-Fi, outlets along the back wall, and nobody will hurry you along. The one ask: between 8 and 9 on weekday mornings it gets busy, so if you're settling in for hours, either side of that window is kinder.

How does the subscription work?

One 12 oz bag ships the same week it's roasted, every month. You can pause, skip a month, change the roast, or cancel entirely from the link at the bottom of any email we send you. No phone calls, no retention scripts.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants?

We do — nine accounts locally, with room for a few more. Wholesale includes weekly delivery, staff training, and help dialing in your equipment at no extra cost. Email us and we'll set up a tasting.
