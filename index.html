What we do

Three things, done properly

We keep the menu short on purpose. It's easier to be excellent at a few things than passable at everything.

Small-batch roasting

Green beans from six farms we've bought from for years. Roasted in 12 lb batches every Tuesday, bagged Wednesday, on the shelf Thursday.

The café

Espresso, filter, and a rotating single origin on batch brew. Pastries come from Odell's two blocks over, delivered warm at 6:45 AM.

Wholesale & subscriptions

We supply nine local restaurants and ship a monthly bag to about 400 households. Same roast, same week, delivered to your door.

Sourcing

We know where every bag comes from

Six farms, four countries, and a standing visit every other year. We pay above Fair Trade floor prices because the people growing this coffee are the reason it tastes like anything at all.

Each bag lists the farm, the elevation, the varietal, and the week it was roasted — so you always know exactly how fresh what you're drinking is.
